# Brand Foundation

This page defines the stable brand layer for OrbMarkets. It should be updated only when production evidence shows a consistent product-level pattern.

## Product Role

OrbMarkets presents Solana network data through a fast, technical, high-signal explorer experience. The interface should feel precise, current, and trustworthy under heavy data density.

## Audience

- Solana builders investigating transactions, programs, accounts, validators, and tokens.
- Traders and analysts scanning live network or market activity.
- Support, operations, and infrastructure teams who need reliable state inspection.
- Power users who value speed, dense information, and direct access to raw identifiers.

## Brand Attributes

- Technical: exposes complex data without hiding useful detail.
- Fast: prioritizes search, scanning, and quick navigation.
- Credible: avoids decorative excess and makes data provenance clear.
- Dense but controlled: supports large tables and detail views without visual noise.
- Solana-native: aligns with ecosystem expectations while retaining its own product identity.

## Voice

Use concise, functional UI language. Labels should be direct and data-oriented.

Preferred:

- `Transactions`
- `Latest blocks`
- `Program details`
- `Copy address`
- `No results found`

Avoid:

- Marketing copy inside app surfaces.
- Over-explaining common explorer actions.
- Playful language in error, warning, or transaction states.
